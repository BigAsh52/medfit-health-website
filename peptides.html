Wellness & Peptide Programs

Beyond GLP-1 — peptide therapy for complete wellness

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the building blocks of proteins — that act as signaling molecules in the body. Physician-supervised peptide therapy has emerged as a powerful complement to GLP-1 programs and a standalone wellness tool for those seeking to optimize energy, recovery, and longevity.

Popular peptide categories include:

  • Cellular Energy — NAD+, Glutathione for mitochondrial support
  • Recovery & Healing — BPC-157, TB500 for tissue repair
  • Performance — Sermorelin, CJC/Ipamorelin for growth hormone support
  • Anti-Aging — GHK-Cu, Epithalon for cellular longevity

Why it matters where your peptides come from

Most peptides sold online are labeled "for research use only, not for human consumption" — a legal loophole that lets sellers skip pharmacy oversight, purity testing, and medical supervision entirely. MedFit works differently:

  • Physician evaluation required — every program starts with a real medical evaluation, not a checkout button
  • Valid prescription, every time — nothing ships without a licensed physician's prescription
  • Compounded by licensed 503A/503B pharmacies — under FDA-registered facility standards, not an unregulated lab
  • Sourced and dispensed for human use — not shipped under a "not for human consumption" disclaimer
